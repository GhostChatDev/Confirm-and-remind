Confirm & Remind

Write it down. Confirm what matters. Get reminded.

Confirm & Remind is a responsive notes and reminders app for everyday life. Capture a note, review any tasks or dates the AI suggests, and choose what you want to be reminded about.

AI suggestions never schedule reminders on their own. Your original notes stay intact, and reminders are only created after you confirm them.

The app is designed to work across phones, tablets, and computers, with a simple Today view, searchable notes, upcoming reminders, and browser notifications on supported devices.
